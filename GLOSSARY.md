# Ostadi Plus

Language for the teacher-first learning platform, based on its approved product rules.

## Identity and ownership

**StudentGUID**:
The permanent public identifier of a global student profile, displayed as text or QR and containing no personal information.

**Teacher workspace**:
A teacher-owned context for their courses, relationships, subscription, and associated records, distinct from other teachers' workspaces.

**Connection**:
A relationship between a student and a teacher or center, with acceptance separate from assignment to a course.

## Learning access and content

**Course-access grant**:
A dated authorization for a student to access a course within its specified semester scope, academic cycle, and authorizing context.
_Avoid_: Payment, connection

**Lesson asset**:
A video or PDF associated with a lesson, with its own media-processing and version lifecycle.

**Media version**:
A particular uploaded version of a lesson asset.

## Student finances

**Student payment record**:
A record of a student payment handled outside Ostadi Plus, distinct from course-access authorization and a teacher's SaaS subscription payment.
