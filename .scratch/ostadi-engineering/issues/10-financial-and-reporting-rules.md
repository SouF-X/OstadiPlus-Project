## Question

What unresolved calculation and document rules must be settled before implementing student financial records, teacher subscription billing, and exports/reports?

Preserve external student payments and separate access authorization. Agree precision/rounding, partial-day/date boundaries, reconciliation, event/report vocabulary, financial-document formats/status, and CSV encoding/layout against the approved behavior. Identify legal/document/retention dependencies and purpose-specific decisions before affected activities. Do not add integrated card payments or change subscription/promotion/pilot qualification rules.

Record the chosen rules with examples that can become later workflow acceptance scenarios. This decision is required before financial workflow implementation; it does not block the first protected-learning specification.

Sources: PRD OP-006/013; approved financial/billing and pilot rules.
