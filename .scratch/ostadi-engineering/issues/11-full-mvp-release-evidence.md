## Question

How will the team track and verify every mandatory MVP/release/pilot prerequisite without confusing the first learning milestone with launch readiness?

Translate the approved Sections 18/21/23/24 gates into an evidence and dependency checklist: all day-one workflows, both normal Google Play and Apple App Store releases, required provider/budget/processing/legal decisions, independent/user/qualified security checks, capacity/recovery/compatibility evidence, operations readiness, technical confirmation, and founder approval.

For pending decisions record the OP-002 fields: question/output, source, status, actual owner when known, authority, next action, affected work, priority, and resolution milestone. Assigning the two developers' implementation work is outside this map. Keep existing pilot exit and expansion conditions unchanged. No calendar deadline or sprint duration has been supplied.

Sources: PRD OP-002/014; RD-001/008-013; Sections 18/21/23/24.
